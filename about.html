About Me

Hello! I’m Anthony P. Jimenez, a student who is passionate about technology, web design, and creativity. I enjoy working with computers, building simple websites, and exploring how design and code can work together to create meaningful digital experiences.
I started developing my interest in technology by learning the basics of HTML, CSS, and C#, and I also use Photoshop to enhance my creativity in design. I like experimenting with different ideas and continuously improving my skills through practice and small projects.
Aside from coding, I also enjoy creative activities such as editing, designing, and exploring new styles. For me, creativity plays an important role in problem-solving, especially when designing user-friendly and visually appealing projects.
I am still learning and growing, but I am motivated to improve every day. This portfolio represents my journey, my skills, and my progress as I continue to develop in the field of technology and design.
